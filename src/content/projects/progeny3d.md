Progeny3D is a cross-platform 3D asset viewer and inspector aimed at custom binary formats that show up in specialized asset pipelines. It is written in modern C++20, with sokol_gfx providing a thin cross-platform graphics layer and Dear ImGui driving the inspection UI.

The project is currently in private development. A longer writeup covering goals, architecture, and availability will land here once it is further along.
